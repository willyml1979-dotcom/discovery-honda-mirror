Discover the perfect combination of rugged capability, modern technology, and distinctive styling with the 2021 GMC Canyon Elevation, available at Discovery Honda in Duncan, British Columbia. This versatile midsize pickup truck delivers the practicality of a hardworking GMC with the comfort and refinement of a modern daily driver. Whether you're commuting through the Cowichan Valley, travelling across Vancouver Island, or heading out for a weekend adventure, the Canyon Elevation is built to handle your lifestyle.

The 2021 GMC Canyon Elevation features an athletic exterior design that stands out from traditional midsize pickups. Its distinctive black grille accents, 18-inch gloss-black aluminum wheels, body-coloured exterior details, LED signature lighting, and fog lights give this truck a confident road presence. The functional cargo bed, convenient CornerStep rear bumper, and EZ-Lift and Lower locking tailgate make loading equipment, recreational gear, and everyday supplies easier.

The Canadian-market 2021 GMC Canyon Elevation was offered with several powertrain configurations. The available 3.6-litre V6 produces an impressive 308 horsepower and 275 lb-ft of torque, paired with a smooth-shifting 8-speed automatic transmission. A 2.5-litre four-cylinder gasoline engine producing 200 horsepower and 191 lb-ft of torque was also offered in select configurations.

Available four-wheel drive provides additional traction for wet Vancouver Island highways, gravel roads, and changing seasonal conditions. Properly equipped V6 models can tow up to 3,175 kg (7,000 lb), making the Canyon an excellent choice for hauling utility trailers, small recreational trailers, and outdoor equipment. Actual towing capacity depends on the specific engine, drivetrain, cab configuration, and installed trailering equipment.

For Canadian fuel economy reference, a 2021 GMC Canyon equipped with the 3.6L V6 and four-wheel drive is rated at approximately 14.0 L/100 km city and 9.9 L/100 km highway. Actual consumption varies with vehicle configuration, driving conditions, and load.

Inside the Canyon Elevation, a thoughtfully designed cabin combines practical storage, supportive seating, and contemporary styling. Features include comfortable cloth seating, a power-adjustable driver's seat, automatic climate control, power windows, remote keyless entry, a leather-wrapped steering wheel, and convenient steering-wheel-mounted controls.

The available Crew Cab configuration offers seating for up to five passengers, making the Canyon a practical option for families, tradespeople, and outdoor enthusiasts. Its comfortable interior and manageable midsize dimensions make it equally suited to busy city streets, highway travel, and weekend adventures.

The 2021 GMC Canyon Elevation delivers modern connectivity through the GMC Infotainment System featuring an 8-inch colour touchscreen, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth connectivity, and USB ports. These features make accessing navigation apps, music, phone calls, and compatible smartphone applications simple and convenient.

Additional Elevation amenities include remote engine start, an auto-dimming rearview mirror, and available connected services. Wireless Wi-Fi hotspot functionality may require an active subscription or compatible data plan.

GMC engineered the 2021 Canyon with a comprehensive selection of safety and stability technologies. Standard equipment includes a rear vision camera, StabiliTrak electronic stability control, traction control, four-wheel anti-lock disc brakes, a tire pressure monitoring system, and multiple airbags.

Available driver-assistance features include Forward Collision Alert, Lane Departure Warning, Rear Park Assist, and upgraded camera technology, depending on the vehicle's installed equipment packages. These systems can provide additional awareness during highway driving, parking, and everyday commuting.

From transporting supplies around Duncan to exploring the scenic roads of the Cowichan Valley, the GMC Canyon Elevation offers an appealing balance of capability and everyday usability. Its practical pickup bed accommodates tools, camping equipment, bicycles, and recreational supplies, while its responsive handling and comfortable interior make longer drives enjoyable.
