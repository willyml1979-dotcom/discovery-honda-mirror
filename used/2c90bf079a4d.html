Discover premium comfort, turbocharged performance, and confident all-wheel-drive capability in the 2015 Lexus NX 200t, available at Discovery Honda in Duncan, British Columbia. This sophisticated compact luxury SUV combines striking Lexus design with practical cargo space, modern convenience, and a smooth, composed ride. Whether you are commuting through the Cowichan Valley, travelling to Nanaimo or Victoria, or exploring Vancouver Island, the NX 200t offers a refined way to get there.

The 2015 NX 200t introduced a bold new direction for Lexus SUV styling. Its signature spindle grille, sculpted body lines, pronounced wheel arches, LED low-beam headlights, LED daytime running lights, and distinctive rear lighting create a confident, contemporary appearance. Standard 17-inch aluminum alloy wheels, heated power-adjustable exterior mirrors, a roof-mounted rear spoiler, and chrome-tipped dual exhaust outlets add a premium finishing touch. Its compact dimensions make this Lexus easy to maneuver through Duncan, park downtown, and enjoy on winding Island roads.

Under the hood, the Canadian-market 2015 Lexus NX 200t features a 2.0-litre twin-scroll turbocharged four-cylinder engine producing 235 horsepower and 258 lb-ft of torque. A six-speed automatic transmission delivers smooth shifts, while standard Dynamic Torque Control all-wheel drive distributes power as needed to help maintain traction on wet pavement and changing road surfaces. Selectable Eco, Normal, and Sport drive modes let you tailor the driving experience to your preferences.

Natural Resources Canada's 2015 fuel consumption ratings for the NX 200t AWD are 10.6 L/100 km city, 8.4 L/100 km highway, and 9.6 L/100 km combined. The fuel tank holds approximately 60 litres, and premium gasoline is recommended. With properly specified towing equipment, the NX 200t has a published maximum towing capacity of 907 kg (2,000 lb). Always verify the exact vehicle's towing suitability before hauling a trailer.

Inside, the NX 200t offers a welcoming five-passenger cabin with elegant finishes, supportive NuLuxe seating surfaces, eight-way power-adjustable front seats, dual-zone automatic climate control, a leather-wrapped steering wheel, and a driver-focused dashboard. The quiet, carefully designed interior provides everyday comfort for commuting, shopping, and longer highway trips across Vancouver Island.

A versatile 60/40 split-folding rear seat helps accommodate passengers and larger items. Lexus lists approximately 500 litres of cargo space with the rear seats upright and up to 1,545 litres with them folded. That flexibility makes the NX well suited to luggage, groceries, golf clubs, and weekend recreational gear. Depending on the installed option package, additional luxuries may include heated and ventilated front seats, a heated steering wheel, driver memory settings, a power moonroof, leather seating, and a power liftgate.

The 2015 Lexus NX 200t includes a 7-inch multimedia display, an eight-speaker Lexus audio system, Bluetooth hands-free connectivity, USB audio input, steering-wheel audio controls, a rearview camera, and Smart Key access with push-button start. An easy-to-read multi-information display keeps important vehicle details within view. Select available packages add navigation with Lexus Remote Touch, an upgraded 10-speaker audio system, wireless phone charging, and other premium conveniences. Equipment depends on the specific vehicle.

Lexus designed the NX 200t with multiple airbags, Vehicle Stability Control, traction control, an anti-lock braking system, Brake Assist, Electronic Brake-force Distribution, Hill-start Assist Control, and a tire pressure monitoring system. The standard backup camera assists with reversing and parking. Depending on the equipment package, available technologies include Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert, Lane Departure Alert, a Pre-Collision System, and Dynamic Radar Cruise Control. Confirm optional driver-assistance features on this vehicle before purchase.

With standard AWD, a responsive turbocharged engine, luxury-minded comfort, and practical cargo versatility, the Lexus NX 200t is a smart fit for life on Vancouver Island. It delivers the elevated driving position buyers appreciate in a compact SUV without sacrificing city-friendly proportions.
