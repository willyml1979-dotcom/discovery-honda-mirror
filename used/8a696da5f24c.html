2017 Honda Odyssey SE for Sale in Duncan, BC

Discover why the Honda Odyssey has earned its reputation as one of Canada's favourite family minivans with this well-equipped 2017 Honda Odyssey SE. Combining exceptional versatility, outstanding reliability, and comfortable seating for up to eight passengers, this Odyssey is perfectly suited for growing families, daily commuting, school activities, and road trips across Vancouver Island. Whether you're travelling through Duncan, Victoria, Nanaimo, Courtenay, or heading out for a weekend adventure, the Odyssey delivers the comfort and practicality every family appreciates.

Powering the 2017 Odyssey SE is Honda's proven 3.5-litre i-VTEC V6 engine producing 248 horsepower and 250 lb-ft of torque. Paired with a smooth six-speed automatic transmission and front-wheel drive, the Odyssey offers strong acceleration, confident highway passing power, and refined driving manners while maintaining impressive fuel efficiency for a vehicle of its size. Honda's renowned engineering ensures dependable performance and low ownership costs for years to come.

The exterior features a timeless design with stylish alloy wheels, body-coloured mirrors and door handles, power sliding rear doors for effortless passenger access, privacy glass, and a sleek aerodynamic profile. The wide-opening doors and low step-in height make loading children, passengers, groceries, and cargo exceptionally convenient, while the spacious rear liftgate provides easy access to the generous cargo area.

Inside, the Odyssey SE offers a thoughtfully designed cabin with seating for up to eight passengers. Premium cloth seating, tri-zone automatic climate control, a power-adjustable driver's seat, a leather-wrapped steering wheel, abundant storage compartments, and versatile seating configurations make every trip comfortable. Honda's Magic Seat functionality allows the second and third rows to fold and reconfigure easily, providing exceptional cargo flexibility for everything from sports equipment to camping gear.

Technology keeps everyone connected and entertained with a touchscreen infotainment system, Bluetooth hands-free calling and audio streaming, USB connectivity, multiple power outlets, steering wheel-mounted audio controls, and a rearview camera. The Odyssey SE also includes a rear entertainment system, making long family road trips throughout British Columbia more enjoyable for rear-seat passengers.

Honda places a strong emphasis on safety, and the 2017 Odyssey SE is equipped with numerous advanced safety features. Depending on vehicle configuration, these may include Vehicle Stability Assist with Traction Control, Anti-lock Braking System (ABS), Electronic Brake-force Distribution, Brake Assist, multiple airbags, active front head restraints, a multi-angle rearview camera, and Honda's Advanced Compatibility Engineering (ACE) body structure designed to enhance occupant protection.

Known for its exceptional durability, outstanding resale value, and family-friendly versatility, the 2017 Honda Odyssey continues to be one of the smartest choices in the pre-owned minivan market. Its spacious interior, proven V6 performance, comfortable ride quality, and legendary Honda reliability make it an outstanding vehicle for busy families across Vancouver Island.
