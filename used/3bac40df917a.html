Perfect for Vancouver Island drivers seeking a fuel-efficient luxury sedan, this Accord delivers a refined driving experience whether commuting between Victoria and Nanaimo, exploring the Cowichan Valley, or enjoying longer highway journeys across British Columbia.

The 2023 Honda Accord Touring Hybrid features Honda's fourth-generation two-motor hybrid system, combining a 2.0-litre Atkinson-cycle four-cylinder engine with powerful electric motors. Together, they produce 204 combined horsepower and 247 lb-ft of torque, delivering responsive acceleration, smooth performance, and impressive efficiency.

An electronic continuously variable transmission (e-CVT) provides seamless power delivery, while selectable Normal, ECON, Sport, and Individual driving modes allow drivers to customize their driving experience. Steering wheel-mounted deceleration selectors help manage regenerative braking.

Unlike a plug-in hybrid, the Accord Hybrid does not require external charging. Its intelligent hybrid system automatically manages gasoline and electric power while recovering energy through regenerative braking.

Step inside the beautifully appointed Accord Touring Hybrid and experience a spacious, modern cabin designed for exceptional comfort. Premium perforated leather-trimmed seating surfaces, elegant interior detailing, and refined materials create an upscale atmosphere.

The Touring trim includes heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear outboard seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, an 8-way power-adjustable driver's seat with lumbar support and memory settings, and a 4-way power front passenger seat.

Dual-zone automatic climate control, a one-touch power moonroof, remote engine start, proximity key entry, push-button start, and an auto-dimming rearview mirror provide additional everyday convenience. Rear passengers enjoy generous legroom and dedicated rear ventilation.

The 2023 Accord Touring Hybrid features an impressive 12.3-inch touchscreen infotainment system paired with a customizable 10.2-inch digital instrument display. Google built-in technology provides convenient access to Google Maps, Google Assistant, and compatible applications.

Additional Touring features include rain-sensing windshield wipers, heated power mirrors with integrated turn signals, and an acoustic windshield for reduced road noise.
