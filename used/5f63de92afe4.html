Sport-Inspired Design

Versatile Hatchback Practicality

Comfort and Technology

Smartphone connectivity throughApple CarPlay and Android Autoprovides convenient access to compatible navigation, music, messages and apps through the Display Audio touchscreen.

Sport models also includeHonda LaneWatch, which displays a camera view of the passenger-side blind spot when the right turn signal is activated. CVT-equipped models add steering-wheel-mounted paddle shifters for additional driver control.
