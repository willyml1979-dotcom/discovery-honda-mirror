2.0L Honda Performance

Power comes from a2.0-litre, 16-valve, Port Injection, DOHC i-VTEC 4-cylinder engineproducing158 horsepower at 6,500 rpm and 138 lb-ft of torque at 4,200 rpm. A smooth continuously variable transmission (CVT) provides responsive and efficient performance, while Honda's Eco Assist system and ECON mode help drivers get more out of every litre of fuel.

The 2023 HR-V LX was offered in Canada with either front-wheel drive or Honda's availableReal Time AWD with Intelligent Control System. Canadian fuel economy ratings are9.1 L/100 km city, 7.4 highway and 8.3 combined for FWD, while the LX AWD is rated at9.4 city, 7.8 highway and 8.7 combined.

Comfortable and Practical Interior

Inside, the HR-V LX provides comfortable seating for five with a clean, modern cabin layout. Standard Canadian LX equipment includesheated front seats, automatic climate control, a 6-way manually adjustable driver's seat, 4-way manually adjustable front passenger seat, 60/40 split-folding rear seatback, proximity key entry with pushbutton start and remote engine starter.

Practicality is one of the HR-V's biggest strengths. The cargo area provides approximately691 litres of space behind the rear seats and up to 1,559 litres with the rear seats folded. Whether you're carrying groceries, luggage, sports equipment, camping gear or larger purchases, the HR-V provides impressive flexibility for a compact SUV.

Connectivity and Everyday Technology

The LX features a7-inch colour touchscreenwith wired Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Bluetooth HandsFreeLink, USB connectivity and illuminated steering-wheel-mounted audio controls. A 7-inch colour TFT centre meter display provides important driving information directly in front of the driver.

A multi-angle rearview camera with dynamic guidelines makes reversing and parking easier, while heated power side mirrors and rear privacy-tinted glass add convenience for year-round Vancouver Island driving.

Honda Sensing Safety Technology

The 2023 HR-V incorporates Honda's advanced driver-assistance technology to provide additional confidence on the road. Features includeAdaptive Cruise Control with Low-Speed Follow, Lane Keeping Assist System, Traffic Jam Assist, Traffic Sign Recognition and automatic high beams, along with Honda's collision mitigation and road departure technologies.

Combined with Vehicle Stability Assist, traction control, anti-lock brakes and a multi-angle rearview camera, the HR-V provides a comprehensive approach to passenger protection and driver confidence.

Built for Vancouver Island Lifestyles
