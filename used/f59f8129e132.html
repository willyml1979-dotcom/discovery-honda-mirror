204-Horsepower Two-Motor Hybrid Performance

Under the hood is a2.0-litre, 4-cylinder Atkinson-cycle engine paired with Honda's two-motor hybrid system. Together, the system produces a combined204 horsepower and 247 lb-ft of torque, delivering responsive acceleration while maintaining excellent fuel efficiency. An Electric-Continuously Variable Transmission (E-CVT) provides smooth and seamless power delivery.

The Touring Hybrid comes equipped withReal Time AWD with Intelligent Control System, helping provide additional traction and confidence during Vancouver Island's wet winters and changing road conditions. Four selectable drive modes -- Sport, Normal, ECON and Snow -- allow the driver to tailor vehicle performance to the conditions.

Excellent Canadian Fuel Economy

One of the biggest advantages of the 2024 CR-V Hybrid Touring is its efficiency. Canadian fuel-consumption ratings are approximately6.0 L/100 km city, 6.9 L/100 km highway and 6.4 L/100 km combined. It uses regular unleaded fuel and features a 53-litre fuel tank, making it an excellent option for drivers who want SUV practicality without traditional SUV fuel consumption.

Premium Touring Interior

Step inside and you'll find a refined five-passenger cabin designed around comfort and everyday usability. The Touring Hybrid featuresperforated leather-trimmed seating surfaces, heated front and rear seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, power-adjustable front seating and driver-seat position memory.

A one-touch power moonroof brings additional natural light into the cabin, while dual-zone automatic climate control helps keep passengers comfortable throughout the year. The versatile 60/40 split-folding rear seat makes it easy to switch between passenger and cargo requirements.

Technology & Connectivity

The CR-V Hybrid Touring offers modern connectivity through its touchscreen infotainment system withwireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Wireless smartphone charging helps reduce cable clutter, while Bluetooth, SiriusXM compatibility and multiple USB connections provide convenient entertainment and charging options.

As the premium CR-V trim, the Touring Hybrid also provides an upscale audio experience with aBose premium sound system, making everything from daily commuting to longer Vancouver Island road trips more enjoyable.

Honda Sensing Safety Technology

Honda Sensing driver-assistance technology provides added confidence behind the wheel. Key systems include theCollision Mitigation Braking System, Forward Collision Warning, Lane Departure Warning, Road Departure Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist System and Adaptive Cruise Control with Low-Speed Follow.

Additional technologies include a Blind Spot Information system, Rear Cross Traffic Monitor, auto high beams, Vehicle Stability Assist with Traction Control, Hill Start Assist and Hill Descent Control. Together, these systems make the CR-V well suited to busy city streets, highways and changing Vancouver Island driving conditions.

Versatile Cargo Space

The CR-V Hybrid Touring offers approximately1,028 litres of cargo capacity behind the rear seats. Fold the rear seats and maximum cargo volume expands to approximately2,030 litres, providing plenty of room for luggage, photography equipment, sports gear, shopping or weekend adventures.

The Touring Hybrid also features a hands-free power tailgate with programmable height, making accessing the cargo area easier when your hands are full. Maximum available towing capacity is approximately453 kg (1,000 lb)when properly equipped.
