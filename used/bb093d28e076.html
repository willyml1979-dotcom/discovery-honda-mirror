Performance and Capability

The 2015 GMC Terrain SLT was offered in Canada with a standard2.4-litre direct-injection 4-cylinder engineproducing182 horsepower and 172 lb-ft of torque, paired with a smooth6-speed automatic transmission. Depending on equipment, Terrain models were available with front-wheel drive or all-wheel drive.

The SLT trim adds a more refined feel to the Terrain's spacious five-passenger cabin. Features available or standard depending on the specific SLT configuration includeleather-appointed seating, heated front seats, remote vehicle start, automatic climate control, an 8-way power driver's seat and a leather-wrapped steering wheel. The elevated seating position and generous passenger room make the Terrain a comfortable SUV for both daily driving and longer road trips.
