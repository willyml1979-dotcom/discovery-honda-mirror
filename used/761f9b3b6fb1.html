Elevate your daily drive with this 2025 Mazda CX-30 GT, now available at Discovery Honda in Duncan, British Columbia. Combining premium craftsmanship, responsive performance, advanced technology, and intelligent all-wheel drive, the Mazda CX-30 GT is an exceptional compact SUV for drivers throughout Vancouver Island. Whether you're commuting through Victoria, exploring the Cowichan Valley, or taking weekend trips to the coast or mountains, the CX-30 GT delivers a refined driving experience with the versatility to match your active lifestyle.

Designed using Mazda's signature Kodo: Soul of Motion philosophy, the 2025 CX-30 GT features sleek body lines, a bold front grille, LED lighting, premium alloy wheels, and sculpted styling that gives it the appearance of a luxury European crossover. Its compact dimensions make parking and navigating busy city streets effortless, while the elevated ride height provides excellent visibility and confidence behind the wheel.

Under the hood is Mazda's proven 2.5-litre SKYACTIV-G DOHC 4-cylinder engine producing 191 horsepower and 186 lb-ft of torque. Paired with a responsive 6-speed SKYACTIV-Drive automatic transmission featuring manual shift mode and Sport Mode, the CX-30 offers smooth acceleration, confident passing power, and engaging driving dynamics. Standard i-Activ All-Wheel Drive continuously monitors road conditions and proactively distributes power where it's needed, delivering exceptional traction during rain, snow, gravel roads, and changing weather conditions common across British Columbia.

Engineered for both performance and efficiency, the CX-30 GT offers Canadian fuel economy ratings of approximately 9.0 L/100 km in the city, 7.1 L/100 km on the highway, and 8.1 L/100 km combined, helping reduce fuel stops without sacrificing driving enjoyment.

Inside, the GT trim surrounds occupants with premium comfort and upscale finishes. Leather-trimmed seating surfaces, a power-adjustable driver's seat with memory settings, heated front seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, dual-zone automatic climate control, a power glass moonroof, and high-quality soft-touch materials create a refined cabin that's designed to make every drive more enjoyable.

The thoughtfully designed interior comfortably seats five passengers while providing impressive versatility. The 60/40 split-folding rear seats expand the cargo area to accommodate luggage, groceries, sports equipment, camping gear, or larger everyday items. Clever storage compartments throughout the cabin ensure everything has its place, making the CX-30 equally practical for commuting, road trips, and family adventures.

Technology is another standout feature of the GT trim. A large centre display paired with Mazda Connect provides intuitive access to navigation, entertainment, and vehicle settings. Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Bluetooth hands-free connectivity, wireless phone charging, integrated navigation, USB-C charging ports, SiriusXM capability, and a premium Bose audio system deliver seamless connectivity and exceptional sound quality throughout every journey.

Safety remains one of Mazda's top priorities. The 2025 CX-30 GT comes equipped with Mazda's advanced i-Activsense safety technologies designed to help increase driver awareness and reduce the risk of collisions. Features include Smart Brake Support, Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Blind Spot Monitoring, Rear Cross Traffic Alert, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keep Assist, Driver Attention Alert, Traffic Sign Recognition, High Beam Control, a 360-Degree View Monitor, front and rear parking sensors, Dynamic Stability Control, Traction Control System, Hill Launch Assist, Tire Pressure Monitoring System, and a comprehensive advanced airbag system.

Additional premium equipment includes keyless entry with push-button start, rain-sensing windshield wipers, power-folding heated exterior mirrors, LED daytime running lights, automatic LED headlights, rear privacy glass, premium alloy wheels, and Mazda's refined suspension that provides an outstanding balance of comfort and responsive handling.
