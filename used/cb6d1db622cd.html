Discover premium comfort, outstanding fuel efficiency and advanced technology with the 2024 Honda CR-V Hybrid Touring AWD, available at Discovery Honda in Duncan, British Columbia. This top-of-the-line Canadian CR-V Hybrid combines sophisticated styling, an innovative hybrid powertrain and standard Real Time All-Wheel Drive, making it an excellent choice for Vancouver Island drivers seeking a luxurious, practical and fuel-efficient SUV.

Powered by a 2.0-litre Atkinson-cycle 4-cylinder engine paired with Honda's advanced two-motor hybrid system, the 2024 CR-V Hybrid Touring delivers 204 combined horsepower and 247 lb-ft of torque. Its smooth electronic continuously variable transmission (E-CVT) provides responsive acceleration and refined everyday performance.

Honda's Real Time AWD with Intelligent Control System helps maintain traction during changing road conditions. Four selectable drive modes, including Normal, ECON, Sport and Snow, allow drivers to customize their experience. Steering wheel-mounted deceleration selectors provide additional control over regenerative braking.

The 2024 Honda CR-V Hybrid Touring AWD offers impressive Natural Resources Canada fuel consumption ratings of just 6.0 L/100 km city, 6.9 L/100 km highway and 6.4 L/100 km combined. Its 53-litre fuel tank and efficient hybrid technology make it ideal for commuting throughout the Cowichan Valley, travelling over the Malahat or enjoying longer road trips across Vancouver Island. No external charging is required.

Step inside a spacious five-passenger cabin featuring perforated leather-trimmed seating surfaces with distinctive orange accents. Premium amenities include heated front and rear seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, an 8-way power driver's seat with memory settings, a 4-way power front passenger seat and dual-zone automatic climate control.

A one-touch power moonroof fills the cabin with natural light, while acoustic glass helps create a quieter driving environment. Additional conveniences include proximity key entry, push-button start, remote engine start, an auto-dimming rearview mirror and a hands-free power tailgate with programmable height.

The CR-V Hybrid Touring features a 9-inch touchscreen Display Audio system with integrated navigation, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility, wireless smartphone charging and Bluetooth connectivity. A premium 12-speaker Bose sound system, including a subwoofer, delivers exceptional audio quality.

Additional technology includes a 7-inch digital driver information display, front and rear USB ports, SiriusXM capability, HD Radio and steering wheel-mounted audio and communication controls.

Drive with added confidence thanks to the Honda Sensing suite of advanced safety technologies. Features include Collision Mitigation Braking System, Forward Collision Warning, Road Departure Mitigation, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keeping Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control with Low-Speed Follow, Traffic Jam Assist and Traffic Sign Recognition.

Additional driver-assistance equipment includes Blind Spot Information, Rear Cross Traffic Monitor, front and rear parking sensors, Low-Speed Braking Control, Hill Descent Control and a multi-angle rearview camera with dynamic guidelines.

The Touring Hybrid stands out with 19-inch black aluminum-alloy wheels, LED headlights, black roof rails, a gloss-black front grille, heated power mirrors, rain-sensing windshield wipers and elegant exterior detailing.

Designed for everyday versatility, this CR-V offers 1,028 litres of cargo capacity behind the rear seats and up to 2,030 litres with the 60/40 split-folding rear seats lowered. With 208 mm of ground clearance, it provides a practical combination of passenger comfort, cargo flexibility and all-weather capability.
