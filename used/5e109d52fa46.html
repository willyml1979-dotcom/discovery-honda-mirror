Whether commuting through the Cowichan Valley, travelling to Nanaimo or Victoria, or exploring scenic coastal roads, the HR-V offers confidence, efficiency and flexibility for your daily adventures.

The 2024 Honda HR-V LX-B AWD features a 2.0-litre DOHC i-VTEC 4-cylinder engine producing 158 horsepower at 6,500 rpm and 138 lb-ft of torque at 4,200 rpm. A continuously variable transmission (CVT) helps deliver smooth acceleration, while Honda's Real Time AWD with Intelligent Control System enhances traction when road conditions change. Select Normal, ECON or Snow drive modes to suit your journey. Hill Descent Control, Hill Start Assist and an electronic parking brake with automatic brake hold add everyday convenience.

Official Canadian five-cycle fuel consumption estimates for the 2024 HR-V LX-B AWD are 9.4 L/100 km city, 7.8 L/100 km highway and 8.7 L/100 km combined. This SUV runs on regular gasoline and has a 53-litre fuel tank. Its 17-inch aluminum-alloy wheels, all-season tires, responsive steering and independent multi-link rear suspension make it well suited to both city streets and longer Vancouver Island drives. Actual fuel consumption varies with driving conditions and habits.

The spacious HR-V LX-B offers seating for five, heated front seats, automatic climate control with air filtration, a 6-way manually adjustable driver's seat and a 4-way adjustable front passenger seat. Proximity key entry with push-button start, remote engine start, power windows and a tilt-and-telescoping steering column help simplify daily driving. A 60/40 split-folding rear seatback expands cargo versatility for groceries, sports equipment, camping gear and weekend luggage.

With 691 litres of cargo volume behind the rear seats and up to 1,559 litres with the rear seats folded, the 2024 HR-V delivers impressive practicality in a compact package. Its versatile rear cargo area makes it a great option for busy families, commuters and outdoor enthusiasts throughout Duncan, Parksville, Nanaimo and the Cowichan Valley.

Stay connected through a 7-inch colour touchscreen with wired Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility, Bluetooth hands-free connectivity, a 4-speaker audio system and three USB device connectors. A 7-inch digital driver information display provides key vehicle information, while a multi-angle rearview camera with dynamic guidelines helps when backing into tight parking spaces.

The 2024 Honda HR-V LX-B comes with Honda Sensing safety and driver-assistance technologies, including Collision Mitigation Braking System, Forward Collision Warning, Road Departure Mitigation, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keeping Assist System, Adaptive Cruise Control with Low-Speed Follow, Traffic Jam Assist and Traffic Sign Recognition. Vehicle Stability Assist with Traction Control, anti-lock brakes, multiple airbags and an Advanced Compatibility Engineering body structure provide additional peace of mind. Driver-assistance features have limitations and require an attentive driver.
