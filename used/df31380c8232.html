Under the hood, the Canadian-market 2019 Jeep Renegade Trailhawk features a 1.3-litre MultiAir turbocharged four-cylinder engine delivering 177 horsepower and 200 lb-ft of torque. Approximately 221 mm (8.7 inches) of ground clearance, off-road suspension tuning, skid plates, and improved approach and departure angles help the Trailhawk handle uneven trails and rough access roads.
