2018 Toyota Tacoma TRD Off-Road -- Rugged V6 4x4 Pickup for Vancouver Island

Built for drivers who want proven capability, everyday versatility, and serious off-road performance, this2018 Toyota Tacoma TRD Off-Roadis an excellent choice for life on Vancouver Island. Whether you're commuting through Duncan, heading up-island for the weekend, exploring logging roads around the Cowichan Valley, or hauling gear to your next outdoor adventure, the Tacoma delivers the durability and capability that have made it one of Canada's most sought-after midsize pickup trucks.

Under the hood is Toyota's 3.5-litre DOHC V6 engine producing approximately278 horsepower and 265 lb-ft of torque. The V6 provides strong acceleration, confident highway performance, and the power needed for towing, hauling, and tackling steep Vancouver Island terrain.

TRD Off-Road Capability

The TRD Off-Road model is designed for drivers who venture beyond pavement. Canadian-market TRD Off-Road equipment includes a capable four-wheel-drive system along with specialized off-road hardware designed to improve traction and control on gravel roads, muddy trails, snow, loose surfaces, and uneven terrain. Depending on configuration, TRD Off-Road equipment includes features such as an electronically locking rear differential, protective underbody components, all-terrain-focused tires, Multi-Terrain Select and Crawl Control.

Built for Vancouver Island

The Tacoma's combination of manageable midsize dimensions and genuine truck capability makes it ideal for Vancouver Island. Its 4x4 system provides added confidence during wet winters and trips into the backcountry, while the pickup bed provides plenty of versatility for camping equipment, mountain bikes, tools, landscaping supplies, fishing gear, and weekend projects.

Comfortable and Functional Interior

Inside, the Tacoma combines rugged construction with the comfort and technology needed for daily driving. TRD Off-Road models feature a driver-focused cabin with supportive TRD seating, a leather-wrapped steering wheel, multi-information display and convenient storage throughout. Depending on factory configuration, equipment may include heated front seats, dual-zone automatic climate control, navigation, Bluetooth connectivity, Smart Key with push-button start, power sliding rear window and a touchscreen audio system.

The V6-powered Tacoma was engineered with recreational and work use in mind. Properly equipped Canadian models feature useful towing capability along with equipment such as a Class IV hitch receiver, trailer wiring and Trailer Sway Control. A composite pickup bed provides durable cargo carrying capability, while select TRD Off-Road configurations also include a convenient 120V/400W power outlet in the cargo bed.

Toyota Safety Sense P

For 2018, Toyota added Toyota Safety Sense P technology to the Tacoma lineup. The system includes driver-assistance technologies such as a Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection, Lane Departure Alert, Automatic High Beams and Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, helping provide additional confidence on Vancouver Island highways and longer road trips.
