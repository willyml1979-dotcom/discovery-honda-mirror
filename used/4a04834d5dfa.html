Discover the perfect combination of pickup truck capability, premium comfort, and Honda reliability with this 2017 Honda Ridgeline Touring AWD, available at Discovery Honda in Duncan, BC. Designed for Vancouver Island drivers who want the practicality of a truck without sacrificing everyday comfort, the Ridgeline Touring delivers impressive V6 performance, advanced all-wheel drive, luxury appointments, and innovative cargo solutions.

Whether you're commuting between Victoria and Nanaimo, exploring the Cowichan Valley, towing recreational equipment, or heading out for a weekend adventure, this versatile midsize pickup is ready for your Vancouver Island lifestyle.

Under the hood, the 2017 Honda Ridgeline Touring features a responsive 3.5-litre, 24-valve, direct-injection i-VTEC V6 engine producing 280 horsepower at 6,000 RPM and 262 lb-ft of torque at 4,700 RPM. Paired with a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission, this powertrain provides confident acceleration, impressive towing capability, and refined highway performance.

Honda's intelligent Variable Torque Management (i-VTM4) all-wheel-drive system delivers enhanced traction and stability in changing Vancouver Island weather conditions. Intelligent Traction Management includes Normal, Snow, Mud, and Sand driving modes for additional versatility.

Inside, the Ridgeline Touring offers a spacious five-passenger cabin with upscale materials and exceptional comfort. Perforated leather-trimmed seating, heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear outboard seats, and a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel create an inviting environment throughout the year.

The 10-way power-adjustable driver's seat with memory settings, power front passenger seat, tri-zone automatic climate control, power moonroof, remote engine starter, and proximity key entry with push-button start add convenience to every journey. A 60/40 split lift-up rear seat provides flexible interior cargo storage for tools, luggage, and recreational gear.

Stay connected with Honda's intuitive 8-inch touchscreen Display Audio system featuring Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth connectivity, and Honda Satellite-Linked Navigation with voice recognition.

The Touring trim also includes a premium 540-watt, 8-speaker audio system with subwoofer, SiriusXM compatibility, and the innovative Truck-Bed Audio System, perfect for outdoor gatherings, camping trips, and tailgating. Additional conveniences include multiple USB connections, rain-sensing windshield wipers, LED headlights, and power-folding side mirrors.

The 2017 Honda Ridgeline stands apart with its versatile composite truck bed, innovative dual-action tailgate, and lockable 207-litre In-Bed Trunk with drain plug. The tailgate can open downward like a conventional pickup or swing sideways for easier loading and access.

With a bed length of 1,625 mm and 1,270 mm of width between the wheel wells, the Ridgeline offers impressive cargo flexibility. Integrated LED bed lighting, heavy-duty tie-down cleats, and a 150W/400W in-bed power outlet add convenience for work and recreation.

The 2017 Ridgeline Touring features Honda Sensing driver-assistance technologies designed to support confidence behind the wheel.

Combining the everyday driving comfort of an SUV with the capability of a midsize pickup, the Honda Ridgeline Touring is an excellent choice for families, outdoor enthusiasts, and professionals. Its independent suspension, intelligent AWD, refined cabin, and innovative cargo features make it exceptionally well suited to Vancouver Island roads and recreational activities.
