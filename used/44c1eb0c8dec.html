Turbocharged Performance & Real Time AWD

Under the hood is Honda's1.5-litre DOHC turbocharged 4-cylinder engineproducing190 horsepower at 5,600 rpm and 179 lb-ft of torque from 2,000 to 5,000 rpm. A smooth Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT) works withReal Time AWD with Intelligent Control Systemto provide confident traction on wet pavement, gravel roads and changing Vancouver Island conditions.

Canadian fuel economy is rated at approximately8.7 L/100 km city, 7.4 L/100 km highway and 8.1 L/100 km combined. The CR-V Sport also has a 53-litre fuel tank and approximately 209 mm of ground clearance.

Technology & Connectivity

The 2020 CR-V Sport features a7-inch Display Audio touchscreenwith Apple CarPlay and Android Auto integration, Bluetooth streaming audio, HD Radio and a 180-watt 6-speaker audio system. Front and rear USB ports help keep devices connected and charged while travelling.

Honda Sensing Safety Technology

Honda Sensing provides an impressive collection of driver-assistance features, includingCollision Mitigation Braking System, Forward Collision Warning, Lane Departure Warning, Road Departure Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist System and Adaptive Cruise Control with Low-Speed Follow. Vehicle Stability Assist with Traction Control, automatic high beams and Honda LaneWatch provide additional confidence on busy highways and city streets.

Spacious Interior & Cargo Capacity

The CR-V Sport seats five passengers comfortably and offers excellent flexibility with its easy fold-down 60/40 split rear seatback. Cargo capacity is approximately1,110 litres behind the rear seats and up to 2,146 litres with the rear seats folded, providing plenty of space for luggage, groceries, camping equipment, sports gear and everyday family needs.
