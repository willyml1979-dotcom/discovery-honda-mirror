5.3L EcoTec3 V8 Performance

Under the hood, the 2019 GMC Sierra 1500 Limited SLE features a5.3-litre EcoTec3 V8producing an impressive355 horsepower and 383 lb-ft of torque. Paired with a 6-speed automatic transmission, this proven V8 delivers strong acceleration, confident highway performance and plenty of low-end power for hauling and trailering.

Depending on configuration, the Sierra 1500 Limited was available with rear-wheel drive or four-wheel drive. Four-wheel-drive models provide additional traction for changing weather, gravel roads, worksites and outdoor adventures throughout Vancouver Island.

Serious Towing & Hauling Capability

The Sierra 1500 Limited is designed to work. When properly configured and equipped, Canadian models can tow up to approximately4,264 kg (9,400 lb). SLE Comfort & Convenience

The SLE trim adds comfort and refinement while maintaining the rugged character expected from a GMC pickup. With seating for up to six passengers depending on configuration, the Sierra's Double Cab layout provides useful passenger space while maintaining the practicality of a full-size pickup. The spacious front seating area and smart interior storage make it equally suitable for commuting, long highway drives and demanding workdays.

Classic GMC Truck Styling

The Sierra 1500 Limited carries the bold styling of GMC's proven previous-generation full-size truck. A commanding front grille, strong body lines and substantial proportions give it a traditional pickup appearance, while SLE exterior details add an upscale touch.
