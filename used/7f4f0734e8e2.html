2022 Honda CR-V Touring AWD -- Premium Comfort, Turbocharged Performance & All-Weather Confidence

Discover one of Canada's most popular compact SUVs with this2022 Honda CR-V Touring AWD. Whether commuting through Duncan, travelling to Victoria or Nanaimo, or heading out for a weekend adventure across the Island, this well-equipped Honda SUV is designed to handle everyday life with ease.

Turbocharged Performance & Real Time AWD

Under the hood is Honda's1.5-litre turbocharged direct-injection 4-cylinder engine, producing190 horsepower at 5,600 rpm and 179 lb-ft of torque from 2,000--5,000 rpm. It is paired with a smooth and efficient continuously variable transmission (CVT). The Touring trim comes standard withReal Time AWD with Intelligent Control System, providing added traction and confidence when Vancouver Island weather brings rain, snow or changing road conditions.

Canadian-spec AWD CR-V models are rated at approximately8.7 L/100 km city, 7.4 L/100 km highway and 8.1 L/100 km combined. Regular unleaded fuel is recommended, helping make the CR-V practical for both daily commuting and longer highway trips.

Premium Touring Interior

As one of the most comprehensively equipped models in the 2022 CR-V lineup, theCR-V Touringprovides a refined cabin with premium materials and excellent passenger comfort. Highlights includeleather-trimmed seating, heated front and rear seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, driver's seat memory and dual-zone automatic climate control.

Apower panoramic moonroofcreates an open and airy cabin, while the hands-free power tailgate makes loading groceries, luggage, photography equipment or outdoor gear easier. The flexible 60/40 split-folding rear seats provide generous cargo versatility for everything from everyday errands to Vancouver Island road trips.

Technology & Connectivity

The Touring trim delivers an impressive collection of technology, including a7-inch Display Audio touchscreen, Honda Satellite-Linked Navigation System, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth connectivity and wireless smartphone charging. A premium330-watt audio system with nine speakers including a subwooferprovides excellent sound throughout the cabin.

Convenience features such asremote engine start, proximity key entry with push-button start, rain-sensing windshield wipers, auto-dimming rearview mirror and steering-wheel-mounted controlsmake the CR-V Touring comfortable and easy to live with year-round.

Honda Sensing Safety Technology

The 2022 Honda CR-V Touring includes an extensive collection of Honda safety and driver-assistance technologies. Features includeCollision Mitigation Braking System, Forward Collision Warning, Lane Departure Warning, Road Departure Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist System and Adaptive Cruise Control with Low-Speed Follow.

The Touring also features aBlind Spot Information System with Rear Cross Traffic Monitor, helping provide additional awareness when changing lanes or backing out of parking spaces. A multi-angle rearview camera further assists with everyday parking and manoeuvring.

Designed for Vancouver Island

With its combination ofReal Time AWD, turbocharged performance, impressive fuel economy, spacious five-passenger interior and premium Touring features, this 2022 Honda CR-V is particularly well suited to life on Vancouver Island.
