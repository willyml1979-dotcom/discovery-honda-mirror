Discover the perfect combination of sporty styling, everyday versatility, and Honda reliability with this 2024 Honda HR-V Sport, now available at Discovery Honda in Duncan, BC. Designed for life on Vancouver Island, the HR-V Sport offers confident performance, exceptional fuel efficiency, advanced safety technology, and a spacious interior that makes every commute, weekend getaway, or family adventure more enjoyable. Whether you're navigating city streets, exploring scenic coastal highways, or heading into the mountains, the HR-V Sport is built to keep up with your active lifestyle.

Powered by a responsive 2.0-litre 4-cylinder i-VTEC engine producing 158 horsepower and 138 lb-ft of torque, the 2024 HR-V Sport delivers smooth, efficient performance through Honda's refined continuously variable transmission (CVT). Available Real Time AWD with Intelligent Control System provides added confidence during wet coastal weather and winter driving conditions, while the HR-V achieves impressive fuel economy ratings of approximately 9.4 L/100 km city and 7.8 L/100 km highway for AWD models, helping you spend more time on the road and less time at the fuel pump.

The Sport trim stands out with bold exterior styling that includes 18-inch alloy wheels, gloss black exterior accents, LED headlights and taillights, heated power side mirrors, and a sleek, athletic stance. Inside, you'll find a thoughtfully designed cabin with heated front seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, premium cloth seating with orange contrast stitching, automatic climate control, remote engine start, push-button start, and a spacious interior that comfortably accommodates both passengers and cargo.

Stay connected wherever your journey takes you with a 7-inch touchscreen infotainment system featuring wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto integration, Bluetooth hands-free connectivity, multiple USB charging ports, and steering wheel-mounted audio controls. The versatile rear cargo area and 60/40 split-folding rear seats make it easy to transport everything from groceries and sports equipment to camping gear and luggage.

Honda Sensing technologies come standard on the 2024 HR-V Sport, providing an impressive suite of advanced driver-assistance features. These include Collision Mitigation Braking System, Road Departure Mitigation System, Adaptive Cruise Control with Low-Speed Follow, Lane Keeping Assist System, Traffic Sign Recognition, Forward Collision Warning, Lane Departure Warning, Auto High-Beam Headlights, and a multi-angle rearview camera to help increase confidence behind the wheel.
