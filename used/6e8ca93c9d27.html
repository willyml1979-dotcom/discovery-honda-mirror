Discover exceptional versatility, comfort, and fuel efficiency with the 2016 Honda HR-V EX-L Navi AWD, available at Discovery Honda in Duncan, British Columbia. This well-equipped compact SUV combines Honda engineering, premium interior appointments, advanced safety features, and practical cargo space. Whether commuting through the Cowichan Valley, travelling to Victoria or Nanaimo, or exploring Vancouver Island, the HR-V delivers a comfortable and confident driving experience.

Powered by a 1.8-litre SOHC i-VTEC 4-cylinder engine producing 141 horsepower at 6,500 rpm and 127 lb-ft of torque at 4,300 rpm, the 2016 HR-V offers responsive everyday performance. Its continuously variable transmission (CVT) provides smooth acceleration, while Honda Real Time AWD with Intelligent Control System helps optimize traction during changing road conditions. Steering-wheel-mounted paddle shifters and ECON mode add flexibility and efficiency.

The 2016 Honda HR-V EX-L Navi AWD delivers impressive fuel economy for a versatile all-wheel-drive SUV. Natural Resources Canada ratings are approximately 8.8 L/100 km city, 7.2 L/100 km highway, and 8.1 L/100 km combined. The 50-litre fuel tank uses regular unleaded gasoline, helping make this Honda an economical choice for Vancouver Island commuters.

Inside, the HR-V EX-L Navi features leather-trimmed seating surfaces, heated front seats, a leather-wrapped steering wheel, leather shift knob, dual-zone automatic climate control, and a power moonroof. Smart Entry with push-button start provides everyday convenience, while the spacious five-passenger cabin offers excellent comfort for commuting, family travel, and weekend adventures.

Stay connected with the Honda Satellite-Linked Navigation System, touchscreen Display Audio, Bluetooth hands-free connectivity and audio streaming, HondaLink, SiriusXM capability, HD Radio, USB connectivity, and a six-speaker audio system. Steering-wheel-mounted controls provide convenient access to entertainment and communication features.

The 2016 Honda HR-V EX-L Navi includes Forward Collision Warning, Lane Departure Warning, Honda LaneWatch blind spot display, a multi-angle rearview camera, Vehicle Stability Assist with traction control, anti-lock brakes, electronic brake-force distribution, Brake Assist, Hill Start Assist, and multiple airbags. Honda's Advanced Compatibility Engineering body structure provides additional occupant protection.

Honda's innovative 60/40 split second-row Magic Seat system provides outstanding flexibility for passengers and cargo. With the rear seats upright, the AWD EX-L Navi offers approximately 657 litres of cargo capacity, expanding to approximately 1,583 litres with the rear seats folded. Transport groceries, camping gear, sporting equipment, or luggage with ease. Additional exterior highlights include 17-inch aluminum-alloy wheels, roof rails, fog lights, heated power mirrors, rear privacy glass, and a rear roofline spoiler.
