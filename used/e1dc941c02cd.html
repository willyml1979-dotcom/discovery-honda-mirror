Turbocharged Performance & Real Time AWD

Power comes from Honda's1.5-litre Direct Injection DOHC turbocharged 4-cylinder engine, producing190 horsepower at 5,600 rpm and 179 lb-ft of torque from 2,000--5,000 rpm. A smooth and efficient continuously variable transmission (CVT) delivers responsive performance while Honda'sReal Time AWD with Intelligent Control Systemprovides added traction when road and weather conditions change.

This combination makes the CR-V Touring particularly well suited to Vancouver Island driving, whether navigating rainy roads around Duncan, travelling over the Malahat toward Victoria, heading north to Nanaimo and Courtenay or setting out on a weekend road trip.

Premium Touring Interior

The Touring cabin offers an upscale environment designed around passenger comfort and convenience. Premium features includeleather-trimmed seating, heated front and rear seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, driver's seat memory, power-adjustable front seating and dual-zone automatic climate control. A power panoramic moonroof brings additional natural light into the cabin, while the hands-free power tailgate makes loading groceries, luggage and recreational equipment easier.

The CR-V's versatile interior provides comfortable seating for up to five passengers along with a flexible 60/40 split-folding rear seat. With the rear seats folded, the spacious cargo area makes it easy to carry camping equipment, sports gear, photography equipment or larger purchases.

Technology & Connectivity

The 2022 CR-V Touring provides an impressive selection of technology, including a7-inch Display Audio touchscreen, Honda Satellite-Linked Navigation System, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth connectivity, SiriusXM, wireless smartphone charging and a premium audio system. Convenient steering-wheel-mounted controls help keep commonly used functions within easy reach.

Honda Sensing Safety Technology

Advanced Honda Sensing driver-assistance technology helps provide additional confidence on busy city streets and longer highway drives. Features includeCollision Mitigation Braking System, Forward Collision Warning, Lane Departure Warning, Road Departure Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist System and Adaptive Cruise Control with Low-Speed Follow. Blind Spot Information and Rear Cross Traffic Monitor provide additional assistance when changing lanes or reversing from parking spaces.

Designed for Vancouver Island

With its combination of Real Time AWD, turbocharged performance, comfortable ride and practical dimensions, the CR-V Touring is an excellent match for Vancouver Island lifestyles. Its manageable size works well around Duncan and Victoria, while its passenger space, cargo versatility and all-weather capability make it equally comfortable on longer trips to Nanaimo, Tofino, Campbell River and beyond.

The exterior is complemented by distinctive19-inch aluminum-alloy wheels, LED headlights, LED fog lights, roof rails, heated power mirrors and a panoramic moonroof, giving the Touring a sophisticated appearance while maintaining the practical character that has made the Honda CR-V a popular Canadian compact SUV.
